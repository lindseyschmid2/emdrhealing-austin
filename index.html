What to Expect:

Is EMDR the right fit for me?

  • Have you experienced any degree of trauma in your life?
  • Do you suffer from anxiety, sleep problems, guilt, anger, fear, or depression?
  • Do you experience looping thoughts or rumination on a previous event that happened in your life?

If you answered yes to any of these questions, EMDR might be a good fit to help you heal toward inner harmony.
