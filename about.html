About Lindsey Schmid, LPC-A

EMDR and trauma-informed therapy with a collaborative, gentle, and solution-focused approach.

I’m so glad you’re here

Looking for a therapist can feel overwhelming and daunting. I help ease your mind and work with adults and older teens experiencing anxiety, depression, low confidence, low self-esteem, and the after-effects of traumatic experiences.

Approach

My approach to helping others heal is collaborative, solution-focused, intuitive, and gentle. I believe people change when they are ready to heal their inner world — such as self-confidence, self-worth, and self-love — which in turn repairs their outer world, like relationships, career, and family.

I specialize in healing trauma through EMDR, which can be extremely effective at reducing fear, anger, guilt, and sleep problems. My approach is different from most therapists because at the core of my personable nature, I care about your results and understand your desire to relieve emotional distress and pain.

What to expect in therapy

I embody a holistic approach in learning about my clients and how we can collaborate toward healing. In our first session, I listen to your history, your triggers, what excites you, and most importantly, what is causing you the most suffering. Depending on your needs, I may provide real-time calming tools after just the first session. Together, we decide on a path forward to meet your goals within our therapeutic sessions.

Lindsey’s style

My style feels grounded and calm, with a touch of relatability. I work from a trauma-informed care lens, honing in on solution-focused efforts.
