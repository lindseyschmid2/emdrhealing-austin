EMDR can help you process overwhelming experiences and move toward relief with more steadiness and clarity.

What EMDR is

Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing, or EMDR, is a structured therapy that helps the brain reprocess experiences that feel stuck, painful, or emotionally overwhelming.

It can be especially helpful for trauma, anxiety, panic, intrusive memories, and the lingering effects of difficult life events.

How it can help

  • Reduce the intensity of traumatic memories
  • Support calmer thinking and stronger emotional regulation
  • Help you feel more grounded in the present
  • Create space for healing without forcing it
